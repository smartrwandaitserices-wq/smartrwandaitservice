We already have an IT person. Why would we need you?

Most of our best clients started with internal IT. We work alongside your person as a co-managed team: we carry the 24/7 monitoring, the after-hours and weekend load, the security stack and the roadmap, and they keep the parts they know best. A 20-minute assessment shows whether the current setup covers everything, especially on security and compliance.

Are we too small for managed IT?

Companies with 10 to 50 people are the primary target for attackers, because they assume no one is watching. Our plans are priced per user, so a 12-person firm pays for 12 users, not for an enterprise contract.

What does it cost?

There are three managed IT plans, billed per user per month: Help Desk Connect, Technology Advantage and Total Advantage. Secure Advantage (cybersecurity) and Cloud Advantage (managed cloud) can be added to any plan or bought on their own. There is a one-time installation fee for onboarding your environment, and any equipment we supply is quoted separately with our mark-up shown on the proposal. The first meeting covers your current setup, endpoints and needs, and the proposal follows from that.

We had a bad experience with an MSP. What is different here?

Three things. Dedicated teams instead of one person wearing every hat: monitoring, help desk, field engineering, projects and a named account manager. Local engineers who come on site from one of 21 offices. And a 60-day money-back guarantee on Help Desk Connect, Technology Advantage and Total Advantage, so you can evaluate us against specific criteria before you commit.

How fast do you actually answer?

A live, US-based service specialist answers the phone in about a minute (1 minute 7 seconds on our latest scorecard), 24 hours a day, 365 days a year. Most issues are handled in that same session.

Do you come on site, or is everything remote?

Both. Remote resolution is fastest for most issues, and field engineers come on site from the nearest of our 21 offices when on-site resources are required. Your account manager runs quarterly business reviews, a technology roadmap and monthly reports.
